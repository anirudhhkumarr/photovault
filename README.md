# PhotoVault

PhotoVault is a private, lightweight photo library that backs up and organizes your photos directly in your personal Google Drive.

[Launch PhotoVault](https://anirudhhkumarr.github.io/photovault)

---

## Highlights

- **Direct Google Drive Sync:** Your photos are saved directly into your own Google Drive (`Photo Vault` folder). Your data stays entirely in your control.
- **Original Quality:** Every photo is stored in full resolution with all camera metadata (EXIF) preserved.
- **Smart Deduplication:** Automatically detects and skips duplicate uploads to save cloud storage.
- **Private & Client-Side:** Runs entirely in your browser with zero third-party servers.
