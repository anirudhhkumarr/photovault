Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Operation

PhotoVault is an entirely client-side web application. All photo processing, scene analysis, and compression happen directly inside your web browser using WebCodecs and local GPU hardware. No photos, files, or metadata are ever transmitted to any third-party servers.

2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Storage

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. We have no tracking cookies, no advertising trackers, and no external analytics.

3. Google Drive Data Access

When you connect Google Drive, PhotoVault uses Google's standard OAuth 2.0 protocol solely to:

  • Upload and backup your photo containers directly to your personal Google Drive storage under the Photo Vault folder.
  • Restore and view your backed-up photo containers in your browser.

Your authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Neither PhotoVault nor its creators have access to your Google account, credentials, or private Drive files.

4. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.
